  • 2025.09 - 2026.05
    Graduate Researcher, LLM Safety and Evaluation (advised by Dr. Jonathan Rusert)
    Purdue University Fort Wayne
    Controlled study of how LoRA, QLoRA, 4-bit quantization, and full fine-tuning affect jailbreak robustness across five open-weight LLM families, with six attacks on HarmBench and JailbreakBench and three automated judges checked against 750 blind human annotations.
    • Used a pre-specified ±5 pp TOST to show that LoRA and full fine-tuning had equivalent black-box safety degradation under the controlled recipe; QLoRA was more variable, and 4-bit quantization alone showed no meaningful degradation.
    • Found that evaluator choice could change the conclusion as much as the adaptation method: GPT-4o-mini mislabeled 45.7% of human-consensus safe outputs as jailbreaks (8.6% for HB-Cls), inflating measured degradation by about 3x.
    • For the JUDGe study, showed that GPT-5.6 judge configurations still over-flagged 21.5–29.9% of safe completions, and identical outputs received different labels on 3–15% of repeat evaluations.
